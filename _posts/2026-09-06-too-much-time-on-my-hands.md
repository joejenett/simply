_(09/06/26, 7:04 AM: written in the throes of an extended internet outage)_  

When you live on the web and the connection stops, watch out!  

The daily challenges keeping your mind fit suddenly disappear. Mind wanders through this new condition realizing you have more time on your hands than you want. Catching up with household chores becomes the new now. Damn! I’m bored!  

The hub is due for an update. A big button wall wants some attention. Links need to be checked. But nothing will happen today.  

To say the least, this is excruciating!
